1. Unique Habitat: Earth is the only planet in the solar system that supports life, with a diverse range of species.
  2. Composition: It is a rocky planet with a dense atmosphere composed mainly of oxygen, carbon dioxide, and nitrogen, which is essential for plant growth.
  3. Size and Shape: Earth is the third planet from the sun and is almost spherical, with a diameter of about 12,757 km (7,925 mi).
  4. Natural Satellite: Earth has one natural satellite, the Moon, which helps maintain the planet's axial tilt and causes the seasons.
